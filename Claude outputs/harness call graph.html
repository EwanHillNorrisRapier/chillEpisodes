Harness Call Graph

Only the 41 procedures and functions, and only the arrows where one executes or calls another. Tables are left out, because almost everything writes to the log and that makes everything look connected.

Thirteen independent pieces, and really five families. Taken literally, the call graph splits into 13 components: one block of 29 and twelve procedures that neither call anything nor are called.

That block of 29 is held together by one object. Nine of the eleven arrows that cross between families are just -> CSPStreamLogger. Set the logger aside and the block falls into 20 pieces, which is the honest picture: the engine is one tight cluster of 19, and almost everything else stands alone.

Nothing here is unreachable by accident. The four families outside the engine are entered from somewhere the harness cannot see: a graph node's own SQL, or a person at a query window. Content file intake is the exception — those two procedures share no object with anything else, not even the log, and would lift out whole.
